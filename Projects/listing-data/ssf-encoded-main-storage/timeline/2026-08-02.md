## Update

Uhh so overflow detection or handling is gonna be super annoying we don’t have a way to take item out of a container automatically and is reusable. My opinions are 

1. Constantly check if a cart at the end of each row is empty, if it’s not it triggers something to stop unloading the cart at the start, send it to the beck of the hall to collect the remaining items and then send it to overflow. I don’t like this very much because that’s like 32 extra carts sitting around and I’d rather not have that. Also just the logistics of it is annoying.. 

2. I just loop the hoppers back around to the start and then do the same logic as above, has all the same problems just without needing to send the cart to the back, plus the hall can be in non entity possessing chunks so it leaves more useable space in those 3x3 chunks. 

3. I just don’t detect it at all and loop the hoppers back into some dedicated overflow management.. again 32 hopper lines hundreds of blocks long in total seems like a bad idea.. 

4. (My personal favourite I think) I take all 4 hopper lines of each side and put them into a cart, every so often it will check if the cart has any empty slots by adding a filter item and trying to remove it again, if it does remove it we know it’s full and can be sent to overflow and replaced with another cart, if it does remove it we know there’s still space for any other potential item types to be overflowing. It’s not at all perfect non of these are but honestly I’m at a loss for how to optimally do this one part…
