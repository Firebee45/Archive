![cover](assets/media/area_render_5_.png)

Version: java
Tags: SSF, Farm

A dripstone farm that is made for SSF

## Features
- 1200/h
- works for sulfur spikes too but rates untested

## Downsides

- needs 2 trident drowned
- fairly big
