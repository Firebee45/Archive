# 8x8x8 Sugar Cane Farm
![cover](assets/media/area_render_20_.png)

Version: Java
Tags: Farm, Crop, Plant

A sugar cane farm that was made for OneChunk

## Features
- 43 sugar cane plants to be harvested
- No static entities
- Speed untested as of now

## Downsides
- Not all sugar cane is collected 100% of the time

## Notes
2 walls are needed in the case of OneChunk this is the world border
