# 8gt Boat Sorter V1.0

![cover](assets/media/area_render_128_.png)

Version: Bedrock
Tags: Storage, Non-Stackable

Sorts boats separately depending on seating positions.

## Features
- Size: 4 x 9 x 4 (144)
- 8gt (hopper speed - 9,000 items/h)
- Non boat item proof*
- Can be FHL
- No non sorted buffer

## Downsides
- **Locational** only works from 256 - 1023, 4096 - 16383, 65536 - 262143 and every other power of two after that.
- Can not handle items such as liquids, tnt, tridents, arrows, splash/lingering portions, sheers, flint and steal, brushes and any other items that have an iteration with dispensers with the exception of things like carts. 
- 3 entities

## Notes
When loading the structure the sorter will break in many places. Please ensure it's chunk aligned and fixed according to the world download.
