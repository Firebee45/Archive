## Notes
These are directional due to how momentum is transferred when snapping to a rail in the opposite direction. For all checkers except **15-1**, the green output must face **North** or **East**. **15-1** cannot be rotated and its green output must face **East**. Each checker runs at its own speed. The values below are measured in **game ticks (gt)** and represent the minimum time between carts. Any interval greater than the minimum also works.
